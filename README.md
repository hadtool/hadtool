## 👋 About me

I'm **Hadiyatullo** — a 3rd-year **Applied Mathematics & Computer Science** student at **Saint Petersburg State University of Industrial Technologies and Design** (class of 2028), originally from **Isfara, Tajikistan** 🇹🇯. Hackathon winner and olympiad medalist.

I like building things end-to-end: collecting and cleaning the data, designing the backend, wiring in AI, and shipping something real people use — Telegram bots, web apps and data tools. Alongside my studies I take freelance work in Python, backend and data.

- 🌱 Currently building an **AI speaking & IELTS trainer** (Next.js + FastAPI)
- 🏝️ Also working on **Islands** — a personal planner that scores your day and turns life goals into islands
